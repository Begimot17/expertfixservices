## 8. Q&A — задать самому и сразу ответить

Google разрешает владельцу задавать вопросы. Это бесплатный способ занять место в профиле правильными ответами, пока их не заняли чужие.

**Q:** Do you provide free quotes?
**A:** Yes. Send us a photo of the job on WhatsApp and we'll come back with a price, usually within the hour. On-site quotes are also free and take about 20 minutes.

**Q:** Which areas do you cover?
**A:** Dublin and the Greater Dublin Area, including Rathmines, Blackrock, Dún Laoghaire, Swords, Malahide, Clontarf, Lucan, Tallaght, Bray and Maynooth.

**Q:** Are you insured?
**A:** Yes, we carry full public liability insurance. We're happy to send the certificate over before any work starts.

**Q:** Do you take on small jobs?
**A:** Yes. Handyman work is booked in a two-hour minimum slot. If you have several small jobs, group them into one visit — it works out much cheaper than separate call-outs.

**Q:** How soon can you start?
**A:** Small jobs usually within 3–5 working days. Larger renovations are scheduled after a site visit.

**Q:** What's the best way to get in touch?
**A:** WhatsApp — it's the fastest. Send a description or a photo of the job and we'll reply with a price, usually within the hour.
